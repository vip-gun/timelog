Declaring on-call

Open On-call. Days are grouped by rotation week; a week runs from {{ week_start_name }} at {{ week_handover }} to the following {{ week_end_name }}. Days are booked whole.

  • Whole week: use the week switch (books all 7 days).
  • Single days: tick the days.
  • Week over two months: the other month's days go on that month's sheet. If that sheet is already submitted or approved they are skipped, with a warning.

Press Save draft. Day type (weekday, Saturday, Sunday, holiday) is automatic.

Two people cannot cover the same day. You are never blocked, but your manager is alerted.

{% if user.is_expert %}

Interventions

If you were called out, tick Intervention on that day and add the incident number and a comment. The day is paid at double rate, whatever the duration.

{% else %}

If you were called

Submitting

Submit once per month from {{ "either the On-call or the Overtime page" if oncall_enabled and not user.is_expert else ("the On-call page" if oncall_enabled else "the Overtime page") }}: every editable sheet of that month is sent together.

  1. Check the totals.
  2. Add a comment for your manager if useful.
    3. {% if features.signature %}
  3. Sign (draw or upload a picture) and tick the confirmation. Remember this signature pre-fills it next time; Forget it removes it.
    4. {% endif %}
  4. Press Submit.

All or nothing: if one sheet has a problem, nothing is sent and the page says what to fix. Until it is decided, Withdraw submission brings it back to draft.

StatusMeaningWhat you do

Messages about your sheets appear as banners until you dismiss them (and by e-mail when configured).

For managers

You review everyone below you, including their reports (skip-level, e.g. when their manager is away). Nobody approves their own sheet.

  1. Open Approvals (oldest first), check the sheet, then Approve, or Reject with a comment.
  2. Statistics shows your team's status and totals (no downloads: Finance reports come from IT Procurement).
  • On-call conflicts: a red banner marks days another employee also has on a submitted or approved sheet. Approval is not blocked; you decide.
  • Pre-approved weekend/holiday overtime: only for work you agreed.
  • Late approval (after the cut-off): paid with the next payroll; the employee is told.
  • Some teams do overtime only; you will see no on-call sheets for them{% if user.is_admin %} (Teams){% endif %}.

Deadlines

A sheet approved by the cut-off date of its month is paid with that month. Later approvals are late claims, paid with the next payroll (you are notified; nothing is lost). Submit early so your manager has time.
