Declaring on-call

Open On-call. The page shows one month, with the days grouped by rotation week. Cover rotates weekly: a week starts on {{ week_start_name }} at {{ week_handover }} and ends the following {{ week_end_name }}. Days are booked whole; the handover time is only information.

Book the days

  • A whole week: use the switch in the left column of that week. It books all seven days at once.
  • Single days: tick the box of each day you were on call.
  • A week that spans two months: the switch books the days of the other month on that month's sheet too. If that other sheet is already submitted or approved, those days are skipped and you get a warning; ask your manager (or an administrator) how to correct it.

Press Save draft. The page shows the number of days and, when your organisation shows amounts to employees, the stipend per day. Each day is classed automatically as weekday, Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday; a holiday that falls on a weekend counts as a holiday.

NoteTwo people cannot be on call at the same time. You are never blocked, but your manager is alerted if a colleague has the same day on a submitted or approved sheet.

What the application does for you

  • Night hours ({{ night }}) are worked out automatically and shown separately from day hours.
  • Past midnight: an entry that crosses midnight is split into one entry per day. If the second part would fall in the next month, enter it on that month's sheet instead.
  • Overlaps: two entries of yours cannot overlap, and the start and end cannot be equal.
  • Day type (weekday, Saturday, Sunday, legal holiday) is set from the date.
    • {% if features.til %}
  • Time in lieu is credited with a factor that depends on the day type and on day or night; the table under your entries shows it.
    • {% endif %}

Weekends and holidays

RequiredOvertime on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday must be either on one of your on-call days (recognised automatically) or marked pre-approved. Otherwise the month cannot be submitted.

Forgot a day in a previous month?

Enter it with its real date: it goes to the sheet of that month.

  • Sheet still a draft or rejected: the entry is added to it.
  • Sheet submitted and waiting: withdraw it first, add the entry, then submit again.
  • Sheet already approved: a supplementary sheet is opened for that month and goes to your manager like any other. It may be paid later (see Deadlines).

Corrections

Entries can be deleted while the sheet is a draft or rejected. Delete the wrong one and add it again.

Called during on-call duty

{% if user.is_expert %}

On the on-call sheet, tick Intervention for that day and add the incident number and a comment. That is all: there is nothing to log elsewhere.

{% else %}

Work you do when called during your on-call duty is declared as overtime, not on the on-call sheet.

  1. Make sure the day is booked on your on-call sheet.
  2. On Overtime, add an entry for the time you actually worked, with the incident number and a description.
  3. The entry is recognised as an on-call intervention automatically (tag On-call), so you do not need pre-approval for weekends and holidays.

The Intervention column of your on-call sheet then shows, read-only, the overtime hours logged on each on-call day, with a link to the entry.

ExpertsExperts record interventions differently (on the on-call sheet, at a double daily rate), so what you see here applies to normal employees.

Submitting

You submit once per month. The Submit panel is on {{ "both the On-call and Overtime pages" if oncall_enabled and not user.is_expert else ("the On-call page" if oncall_enabled else "the Overtime page") }}.

  1. Check the totals on each page.
  2. Add a comment for your manager if useful.
    3. {% if features.signature %}
  3. Sign: draw your signature in the box or upload a picture of it, and tick the confirmation statement. You may tick Remember this signature to have it filled in next time; Forget it removes it.
    4. {% endif %}
  4. Press Submit.

Everything that is still editable for that month ({{ "on-call, " if oncall_enabled }}overtime and any supplementary overtime sheet) is sent together. {% if oncall_enabled %}On the on-call page, Save & submit for validation saves the grid first.{% endif %}

All or nothingIf one sheet has a problem, for example weekend overtime that is neither on-call nor pre-approved, nothing is sent and the page tells you what to fix.

Changed your mind or spotted a mistake? While a sheet is submitted and not yet decided, use Withdraw submission to go back to a draft.

For managers

You review the sheets of everyone below you in the hierarchy: your direct reports and, skip-level, their reports too (for example when their manager is away). Colleagues outside your branch are never visible to you.

  1. Open Approvals: submitted sheets waiting for a decision, oldest first. The dashboard shows how many are waiting.
  2. Open a sheet, check it and press Approve, or Reject with a comment the employee will read. Nobody approves their own sheet.
  3. Statistics shows the status and totals of your team month by month. It has no downloads: the Finance reports come from IT Procurement.

What to look at

  • On-call conflicts: a red banner and highlighted days appear when another employee has the same day on a submitted or approved sheet. Two people cannot cover the same day; approval is not blocked, you decide. Colleagues outside your team are counted, not named.
  • Weekend and holiday overtime is accepted only on an on-call day or when the employee ticked pre-approved: tick it only for work you agreed to.
  • Late claims: an approval after the Finance cut-off (see the dates under Deadlines) is paid with the next payroll. The employee is told automatically.

Deadlines & late claims

Finance processes each month after a cut-off date. A sheet that is approved on or before the cut-off date of its month is paid with the month you worked.

{% for y, m, d, custom in cutoffs %}{% endfor %}
Work monthMust be approved by
{{ month_label(y, m) }}{{ d|date }}

Your manager needs time to approve, so submit as early as you can after the month ends.

  • Submit early in the month after the one you worked, so your manager has time to approve before the cut-off.
  • If the approval comes after the cut-off, or the month was already sent to Finance, the sheet is a late claim and is paid with the next payroll. You are notified. Nothing is lost; it is only paid later.
  • If you submit when the cut-off has already passed, the application warns you straight away.

Questions & problems

{% if not oncall_enabled %}
There is no On-call menu.
Your team ({{ user.team }}) does overtime only. Existing on-call sheets, if you had any, remain visible from the dashboard. Ask an administrator if your team should declare on-call.
{% endif %}
I cannot see an Overtime menu.
Experts do not declare overtime. If you are not an Expert and the menu is missing, ask an administrator to check your profile.
"Weekend/holiday overtime must be on an on-call day or pre-approved."
Either book that day on your on-call sheet, or tick pre-approved on the entry once your manager has agreed to the work.
I cannot add overtime for a month: "withdraw it first".
That month's sheet is submitted and waiting. Withdraw it, add your entry, and submit again.
My time is refused.
Times use {{ step }}-minute steps, the end must differ from the start, the entry must not overlap another of yours, and the date cannot be in the future or more than {{ settings.overtime_lookback_months }} months back.
The days of the other month are missing after I switched on a week.
That other month's sheet was already submitted or approved, so the days were skipped (you were warned). Withdraw that sheet or ask your manager.
My sheet was approved but contains a mistake.
Tell your manager. An administrator can reopen the latest sheet of the month, unless it has already been sent to Finance. For a month already sent, an overtime correction can be filed as supplementary overtime; for anything else, ask your manager.
I do not see any amounts.
Your organisation may hide money amounts from employees. Hours and time in lieu are always shown.
My manager is away.
Any manager above yours in the hierarchy, or an administrator, can approve your sheet; ask them.

Anything else: contact your line manager or the application administrators.
