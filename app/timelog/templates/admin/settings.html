Payroll cut-off

This is the default pattern, used for every month without an explicit date. Finance usually sets one specific date per month: enter them in the cut-off calendar (also open to IT Procurement). Upcoming cut-offs: {% for y, m, d, custom in cutoff_schedule %}{{ month_label(y, m) }}: {{ d|date }}{% if custom %} (set){% endif %}{{ "; " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}.

Sheets approved up to the cut-off of a payroll month are paid with it (default pattern: this day of the following month, generally the 5th). Later approvals, and late submissions, are paid with the following month, and the employee is notified. IT Procurement closes a period by recording that the reports were sent to Finance (Payroll page); from then on nothing more is added to it.

On-call rotation

The rotation runs from this day and time to the same day and time a week later (default: Friday 18:00, so a week is booked Friday to Thursday). The week switch on the on-call sheet books those seven days; the handover time is shown to employees as information, days are always booked whole. Days already booked are not changed.
