Teams

Users & bulk edit

A user's team is a free-text field (filled from the directory for LDAP users). Here you manage the teams themselves: rename one, create an empty one, and decide whether the team declares on-call. A team with on-call switched off does overtime only: its members have no On-call page, no on-call line in the Finance export and no on-call section in their User Guide. Sheets that already exist stay visible. To move people between teams, select them in Users and use Bulk edit.
