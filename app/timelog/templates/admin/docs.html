How it works

  1. Employees fill a monthly draft: on-call days and (normal employees) overtime entries.
  2. One Submit sends all drafts of the month to the line manager (with a comment and, if enabled, a signature).
  3. The manager approves, or rejects with a comment; a rejected sheet is edited and resubmitted.
  4. Approved sheets go into the monthly reports that IT Procurement sends to Finance (Payroll).

Roles & users

RoleCan do
EmployeeDeclares on-call and overtime, submits, withdraws; sees only their own data.
ExpertOn-call only; an intervention doubles the day's rate. Overtime is refused server-side.
Line managerAnyone with direct reports. Approves/rejects everyone below them (skip-level included) and has the Statistics page. No downloads.
IT ProcurementDownloads the whole-organisation payroll reports and records them as sent to Finance. Cannot open individual sheets. Set in Users or by LDAP_GROUP_PROCUREMENT.
AdministratorManages users, teams, grades, holidays, settings; reads the audit log; approves any sheet; reopens an approved sheet (audited).

Colleagues never see each other; unauthorised access answers 404. Team and manager come from the directory (department, manager) at each login unless locked.

  • Users: edit, Archive (disables sign-in, keeps all records, reversible) or Delete (removes the account and its unapproved sheets; refused for users with approved sheets or a recorded payroll run, or for the last admin / yourself).
  • Bulk edit: tick users, fill only the fields to change (team, manager, Expert, grade). Users who would break a rule (hierarchy cycle, Expert in an overtime-only team) are skipped and listed. Lock keeps LDAP users' values from being overwritten at next login.
  • Prepare LDAP users: create directory accounts before their first sign-in from a list of usernames (optional directory lookup; optional team, manager, Expert, grade for all).
  • Teams: create, rename (moves members), delete when empty, and switch on-call off for overtime-only teams. A team with Experts cannot be switched off.
  • Submissions: list every sheet, filter by user, year, kind, status; delete selected sheets permanently (type DELETE). Meant for cleaning up test instances: it also removes lines, signatures and payroll-run links (a run left empty is removed). Audited as sheets_delete; take a backup first.

On-call

  • Weekly rotation from {{ week_start_name }} at {{ week_handover }} (Settings; the handover is informational, days are booked whole). The week switch books all 7 days; a week over two months also books the other month's days on that sheet.
  • Daily stipend from the employee's grade and the day type.
  • Experts record interventions on the grid (incident, comment; double rate). Normal employees record them as overtime, recognised automatically on a booked on-call day.
  • Conflicts: managers and admins see a red banner when another employee has the same day on a submitted or approved sheet. Not blocking; people outside the viewer's scope are not named.
  • Teams without on-call (Teams page): no On-call page, nav item or export line; existing sheets stay visible.
  • Rates and day types are frozen at submission.

Overtime

  • Date + from/to in {{ settings.overtime_step_minutes }}-minute steps; day and night hours ({{ '%02d:%02d' % (settings.night_start // 60, settings.night_start % 60) }}–{{ '%02d:%02d' % (settings.night_end // 60, settings.night_end % 60) }}) are computed. Past-midnight entries are split per day; no overlaps; no future dates.
  • Up to {{ settings.overtime_lookback_months }} months back, on the sheet of the work month. If that month is approved, a supplementary sheet is opened.
  • Weekend/holiday overtime needs an on-call day (automatic) or pre-approved, else submission is refused.
  • Compensation is paid or time in lieu (when enabled in Settings).

Approval

draft ──submit──▶ submitted ──approve──▶ approved
  ▲                 │  └──reject(comment)──▶ rejected ──(edit)──▶ submit again
  └──withdraw───────┘
approved ──reopen (admin, audited)──▶ draft
  • One submission per month covers all drafts; if one sheet is refused, none is sent.
  • Approver: the direct manager, any manager above (skip-level) or an admin; never the employee. Every transition is in the audit log.
  • Signature (Settings): drawn or uploaded, stored with a SHA-256 of the sheet content; managers see whether the content changed since. A simple electronic signature, not a qualified one.

Payroll & Finance

Cut-off. A sheet approved by the cut-off of its work month is paid with that month; later approvals are late claims, paid in the next open period. Cut-offs are explicit dates in the cut-off calendar (IT Procurement or admin); the July row is the date for June work. A month without a date uses day {{ payroll_cutoff }} of the following month (Settings). A period already sent to Finance is closed.

  1. IT Procurement opens Payroll, picks the period and downloads the on-call and overtime reports (whole organisation; late claims get their own "(late)" tab). A third control workbook gives the detail. Files follow the layout Finance uses and hold values, not formulas.
  2. After e-mailing them to Finance, they tick the confirmation and record the payroll as sent. This requires both reports downloaded and still matching the data (if something was approved since, download again).
  3. The sheets are marked sent to Finance, employees are notified, and the period is closed. A sent sheet cannot be reopened.

Employees get a notice (and e-mail if SMTP is set) for late submission, late approval, payroll sent, or a submission still waiting when the period is sent.

SSL / HTTPS

The proxy container (nginx) terminates TLS (TLS 1.2/1.3, HSTS, HTTP redirects to HTTPS); the app is not published. The certificate is configured with files on the server, not from this interface.

File (in certs/)Content
server.crtServer certificate then the intermediate chain, PEM.
server.keyPrivate key, PEM, unencrypted, chmod 600.
ldap-ca.pemCA that signed the domain controllers (LDAPS only; unrelated to the web certificate).

You received a PFX (.pfx / .p12)

Offline package: ./convert-pfx.sh server.pfx (Linux) or .\convert-pfx.ps1 server.pfx (Windows) writes both files; no openssl needed. With OpenSSL:

openssl pkcs12 -in server.pfx -nocerts -nodes -out certs/server.key
openssl pkcs12 -in server.pfx -clcerts -nokeys -out server-leaf.pem
openssl pkcs12 -in server.pfx -cacerts -nokeys -out chain.pem
cat server-leaf.pem chain.pem > certs/server.crt
chmod 600 certs/server.key

OpenSSL 3 error unsupported: add -legacy. Delete the PFX and temporary files afterwards.

You generate the key yourself (recommended)

scripts/make-csr.sh timelog.corp.example.lu [other-dns-name ...]     # certs/server.key + certs/server.csr
certreq -submit -attrib "CertificateTemplate:WebServer" server.csr server.cer   # domain-joined Windows host
openssl x509 -inform DER -in server.cer -out server.pem                          # skip if already PEM
cat server.pem issuing-ca.pem > certs/server.crt

Check and apply

# both lines must print the same hash
openssl x509 -in certs/server.crt -noout -pubkey | openssl sha256
openssl pkey -in certs/server.key -pubout | openssl sha256
openssl x509 -in certs/server.crt -noout -subject -ext subjectAltName -enddate
docker compose restart proxy
  • The SAN must contain the host name users type (SERVER_NAME in .env); clients must trust the root CA (usually via group policy).
  • Not on port 443: set HTTPS_PORT and HTTPS_PORT_SUFFIX=:<port>.
  • Renew by repeating the steps and restarting the proxy (short interruption, no data affected). Dev: scripts/make-selfsigned.sh timelog.local.

LDAP / Active Directory

Local accounts are checked first (a local admin can always sign in); everyone else is checked against the directory. Configure in Admin › Settings › LDAP (stored values override the LDAP_* variables of .env; Reset to .env values removes them).

SettingMeaning / example
Server URLldaps://dc01.corp.example.lu:636, or ldap://… with StartTLS. Never clear text.
CA certificatePaste the root/issuing CA (PEM) or use certs/ldap-ca.pem. Keep server verification on.
Bind DN / passwordRead-only service account. The password is encrypted with a key derived from SECRET_KEY and never shown; rotating the key means re-entering it.
Search base / filterE.g. OU=Users,DC=corp,DC=example,DC=lu and (sAMAccountName={username}) (must contain {username}).
AttributesAD defaults: sAMAccountName, givenName, sn, displayName, mail, department (team), manager.
GroupsDNs for allowed to sign in, administrators, Experts, IT Procurement (nested groups resolved by AD). An empty group means that flag is set by hand in Users.

At each login the service account finds the user, the user's password is checked by bind (empty passwords refused), then names, e-mail, team and manager are updated (the manager account is created too) unless locked, and group flags are applied.

Go live: save, run Test connection (no user password needed), sign in with a directory account in a private window, then set LOCAL_LOGIN_ADMIN_ONLY=true keeping one local break-glass admin.

Typical failures: cannot connect (firewall, port, DNS), certificate verify failed (missing CA, wrong host name), invalid credentials (bind DN/password), no entry (base or filter).

Operations

  • Backup: create, download, upload, delete, restore (writes a safety copy first), and a daily/weekly schedule (UTC, number kept). Files are AES-256-GCM encrypted with a key from BACKUP_PASSPHRASE (else SECRET_KEY): store it separately, a backup cannot be read without it. They live in backups/ next to docker-compose.yml; copy them off the host regularly.
  • Settings switch features: time in lieu, signature on submission, amounts visible to employees, on-call rotation, cut-off day.
  • Logs: docker compose logs -f app proxy. Health: GET /healthz.
  • Update: load the new image (docker load or scripts/build.sh), then docker compose up -d. The footer shows the version (build {{ build.version }}).
  • Secrets are in .env; the app refuses to start with the example placeholders.
